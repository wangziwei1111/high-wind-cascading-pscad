# Stage 13 paper later-stage evidence

No OCR or network source was used; entries come from the existing direct-text evidence registry.

- `E003` line overload protection: Line flow redistributes after outages; an inverse-time overload action opens a line, anchored at 1.1 times limit with 5 s delay. (11 (PDF 19), Eq. 2-6, 2-7)

- `E005` conventional-generator protection: Conventional units use 47.5/51.5 Hz frequency limits with 10 s delay and 0.8/1.3 p.u. voltage limits with 2 s delay. (12 (PDF 20), none)

- `E006` under-frequency load shedding: UFLS is three-stage at 49.0/48.8/48.5 Hz with 0.8/0.5/0.2 s delays and 25/40/55 percent shedding; UVLS is 0.8 p.u., 1 s, 25 percent. (12-13 (PDF 20-21), none)

- `E007` temporary-overvoltage protection: Temporary-overvoltage protection uses a 1.3 p.u. threshold and opens the related line without intentional delay. (13 (PDF 21), none)

- `E010` initial fault type: At 0.50 s a 2 s three-phase fault near bus 29 is followed by wind trip, UFLS, sequential overloaded-line openings, further wind trip, and generator protection. (16-17 (PDF 24-25), Table 2-2)

- `E011` scenario comparison dimensions: The overload-dominant sequence starts with a short circuit, then overloaded line trip(s), topology degradation, source/load actions, islanding and generator loss. (21 (PDF 29), Fig. 3-3)

- `E012` wind-farm / DFIG model: The off-grid-dominant sequence starts with a short circuit, wind trip and power loss, then state/flow redistribution, line trips, load shedding and generator loss. (21-22 (PDF 29-30), Fig. 3-4)

- `E013` evaluation outputs: Evaluation uses line-trip count, wind power lost, load shed, and voltage/frequency severity. (22-23 (PDF 30-31), Eq. 3-1 to 3-5)

- `E019` under-voltage load shedding: UVLS uses a 0.8 p.u. threshold, 1.0 s delay and 25 percent load shedding. (13 (PDF 21), none)

- `E021` wind-farm / DFIG model: The paper models a DFIG wind turbine and uses wind-farm off-grid behavior as the first protection action in an off-grid-dominant chain. (14-15, 21-22 (PDF 22-23, 29-30), Fig. 2-6; Fig. 3-4)
